Shi Lab Volcano & GO

Everything runs in your browser. Your data is never uploaded — only gene and protein names are ever sent out, and only when you ask for annotation or enrichment.

  1. Load an .xlsx, .csv or .tsv results table. Columns are detected automatically; check the mapping if your file is unusual.
  2. Set cutoffs — p-value and |log2 FC|. Counts update live, and you can switch between raw and adjusted p-values.
  3. Hover any point for its protein ID, gene, statistics, subcellular location, pathway and function.
  4. Label proteins by pasting a list, clicking points, or box-selecting a region. Drag labels to place them.
  5. Overlay GO clusters — search an organelle or pathway and every matching protein is recoloured and reshaped.
  6. Run pathway analysis on your up, down or labelled sets, then click a result to annotate its genes on the plot.
  7. Export a 300 dpi PNG or a layered SVG for the figure, plus CSVs of the classification and enrichment tables.
